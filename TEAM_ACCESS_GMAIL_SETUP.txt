AI SDR Dashboard — Team & Access Mock Mode

1. Replace the deployed app.py with the app.py in this package.
2. In Streamlit Community Cloud, open your app's Settings > Secrets.
3. Add the content from secrets.toml.example, replacing the example Gmail address,
   Google App Password, and dashboard URL with your own values.
4. For Gmail SMTP, turn on 2-Step Verification and create a Google App Password.
   Use that App Password, not your regular Gmail password.
5. Save Secrets and allow the app to restart.
6. Open Team & Access, select a campaign, enter the invitee's name and email,
   choose View Only or Can Edit, and send the invitation.
7. The recipient opens the email link and clicks Accept Invitation.
8. Use Switch Mock User in the sidebar to preview the dashboard as that accepted user.

Security note:
- Invitation emails are real.
- Mock user selection and campaign permissions are simulated, not secure authentication.
- Do not use confidential company data until real authentication and shared database
  access controls are implemented.
- Never commit app passwords or other secrets to GitHub.
